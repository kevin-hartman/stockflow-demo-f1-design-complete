## Final reasoning

I wrote five acceptance criteria for S4-adjust-quantity-in-place and added two open questions to `feature-spec.md`. The self-check passes.

**ACs written to `stories/S4-adjust-quantity-in-place/acs/`:**
- **AC1-adjust-updates-row-in-place:** the row shows the new quantity at once without a reload, then shows what the server stored.
- **AC2-adjust-below-zero-rejected:** an adjustment that would go below zero shows a message naming the quantity field and the row goes back to the stored value.
- **AC3-missing-actor-named:** a blank actor shows a message naming the actor field and the quantity doesn't change.
- **AC4-adjustment-history-lookup:** a record's history lists each adjustment with its timestamp, actor, and the quantity before and after.
- **AC5-audit-entries-immutable:** any attempt to edit or delete a history entry is refused and the entry stays as recorded.

**New open questions for the PO:**
- **How a quantity is entered:** as a new total (how AC1 is drafted, and my suggestion) or as a +/- change?
- **Re-filing and history:** re-filing an existing pair in S1 also changes the quantity but doesn't add to the history. Should it? My suggestion is yes, so every quantity change can be traced.
